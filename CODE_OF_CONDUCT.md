# Code of Conduct

ScreenForge aims to be a welcoming, practical and respectful open-source
project.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate of different backgrounds, experience levels and viewpoints.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work rather than the person.
- Assume good intent, acknowledge mistakes and help move discussions forward.
- Respect privacy and keep confidential or sensitive information out of public
  threads.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
trolling and publishing someone else's private information are not accepted.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and other spaces where
someone represents ScreenForge. The maintainer may edit, hide or remove content
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to
protect the community.

For an immediate GitHub-wide safety concern, use GitHub's built-in abuse
reporting. For a project-specific concern, contact the maintainer privately
through the contact channel published on the
[maintainer profile](https://github.com/neogenz). Reports will be handled as
confidentially and fairly as possible.

This policy is informed by the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
